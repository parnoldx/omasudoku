PLAY

Score like an arcade.

Faster play means bigger multipliers. Clear a row, column, or box for a bonus. Keep mistakes under three for a clean series and a shot at the highscore board.

Time multiplier

Your score factor starts high and slowly ticks down. Correct digits pay more while you stay quick.

hurry up for more points

Line bonuses

Finish a row, column, or box and get a little celebration plus extra points on top.

clear a unit · get a ripple

Three strikes

You get three mistakes. Go over and the series is broken — you can keep playing, but that run won’t hit the highscore list.

max 3 mistakes

Autosave & resume

Close the window whenever. Your board, timer, notes, and mistakes are waiting when you come back.

CONTROLS

Stay on the keys.

Move with arrows or vim keys. Enter digits from the number row, numpad, or a left-hand keypad — the same keys also highlight every matching number on the board. Notes, undo, and pause are one key away — no mouse required.

INSTALL

On your Omarchy desktop.

From the project folder, run the installer, then launch the game. It fits your Omarchy look and works fully offline — no accounts, no network.

Terminal Install from the project folder
$ ./install.sh

  1. Run the installer. ./install.sh sets up the launcher and desktop entry.
  2. Launch the game. Run omarchy-sudoku, or find Sudoku in the launcher.
  3. Play offline. Scores stay on your machine. Theme follows Omarchy.
